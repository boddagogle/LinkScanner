## About LinkScanner

LinkScanner helps you scan documents, enhance images, create and manage PDF files, and extract text using OCR — all from your Android device.

## Features

- **Smart Document Scanner** – Capture and enhance document images.
- **Image Enhancement** – Improve document clarity and readability.
- **PDF Creator** – Convert images into PDF documents.
- **PDF Management** – Organize, merge, split, rotate, and manage PDFs.
- **OCR** – Extract text from images and documents in Arabic and English.
- **ID Card Scanner** – Scan the front and back of identification cards.
- **Document Library** – Organize and access your scanned files.
- **Offline-first** – Designed to keep document processing on your device.
